# ADR 005: Better Auth instead of a hosted auth service

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11-10

**Decision.** Better Auth with the organization, bearer and Next.js cookie plugins, on our own PostgreSQL. IDs are
UUIDv7 generated by the app. Better Auth roles only gate its own organization endpoints; business permissions are
decided by `can()` in `packages/core`.

**Consequences.** No per-user cost and the data stays in our database. We own email delivery (outbox) and must keep
the auth tables' schema aligned with Better Auth upgrades.
